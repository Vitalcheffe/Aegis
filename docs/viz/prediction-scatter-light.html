Trajectory Prediction Error vs Horizon · Scatter

Each dot is one prediction trial: x-axis is the prediction horizon (how far into the future), y-axis is the RMSE in meters. The trend line (amber) shows the expected error growth. Below 1.8s horizon, error stays under 20m (engagement threshold, crimson dashed). Beyond 1.8s, error grows non-linearly — the UKF's sigma points can no longer capture the trajectory's curvature. This is why Aegis re-plans every 200ms: never let a prediction age beyond 1.5s.
