Airflow Simulation · Streamlines Around Airfoil

Procedurally generated streamlines around a NACA-style airfoil at Re = 1.2 × 10⁶. Lines colored by local velocity magnitude — amber where flow accelerates over the upper surface, cyan in the slower regions beneath. Particles (violet) are advected along the streamlines to visualize flow direction. The wake region (crimson, dashed) shows where turbulent eddies form downstream — this is the noise source that the UKF must filter when estimating target position from sparse sensor samples.
